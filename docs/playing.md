# Playing Kart64

[Back to Kart64](../README.md)

Open [Kart64](https://eddmann.com/kart64/), press **Start**, and choose your
legally dumped US ROM. The browser checks it and extracts the game's assets
locally. Nothing is uploaded, and the ROM is removed from browser storage
after extraction. The first run takes about a minute; later visits use the
cached game data.

Single-player Grand Prix and Time Trials are supported. Use the keyboard,
a gamepad connected through the browser, or the phone's touch controls.
The [README control table](../README.md#controls) lists the button mappings.

## On a phone

On the menus, a D-pad with **A · OK** and **B · BACK** gets you through mode,
driver, and course selection. Once the race starts, the controls switch to
steering, braking, items, and drifting. **AUTO** keeps the kart accelerating;
turn it off if you want to control acceleration yourself.

On an iPhone, add the page to your Home Screen. Safari can evict extracted
data from ordinary tabs, which means having to extract the ROM again.
The [phone showcase](../media/mario-raceway/README.md) was captured in this
Home Screen mode.

## Tour controls

For a different feel, choose **CTRL: TOUR** in the game's menus or pause menu.
This switches to a one-thumb scheme inspired by Mario Kart Tour:

- Drag anywhere to steer.
- Flick and hold to charge a drift, then release to fire the mini-turbo.
- Swipe up to throw an item forward or down to drop it behind.

Tour clears the brake and action buttons from the view and always fills the
screen while driving in portrait. Rotate to landscape and the pads return.

## Full screen and 4:3

**Full screen** fills the display while you drive, in portrait or landscape.
Menus and results stay 4:3. Use the **FULL** button on a phone to switch between
the full-screen race view and the 4:3 layout.

Full screen is on by default on phones and off on desktop. On desktop, **F**
turns it on together with the browser's own full screen; **Esc** leaves both.
Tour always uses full screen while driving in portrait.

## Custom courses

Beyond the original cups, take the karts to
[Silverstone](../courses/silverstone/README.md), an editable custom course with
a Wing pit shortcut.

1. Start the game normally and choose your mode and driver.
2. Press **Up** from the cup-selection row to open **Custom** inside the game.
3. Browse with the D-pad and confirm twice with **A**.
4. To return to **Original**, press **B**.

Time Trials keeps separate course records and ghosts. Grand Prix offers a
standalone three-lap race against seven CPU rivals, followed by
**Retry / Change Course / Quit**. Original cups retain their usual progression.

The bundled course download is about 4.3 MB and loads at startup. Its optional
preview media is fetched only when viewed. For the editing projects, course
builds, and validation scope, see the [course documentation](../courses/README.md).

For sharper track and character textures, see [texture packs](texture-packs.md).
